Icons in this folder follow the Lucide icon set (https://lucide.dev), outline style, stroke 1.75, 24 px grid.
Most were taken from the design reference's icon sprite (docs/design/ORN_DESIGN_REFERENCE.html, section 4); redo, panel-right and pencil
were added in the same style. mark.svg is an original ORN glyph (hexagon with a ring).
